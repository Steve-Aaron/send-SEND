# Page content update - summary

This note explains the text now used on the campaign page, in plain terms for sharing with the team.

## The page, top to bottom

**Headline area**

- Main heading: 'Tell Parliament Why SEND Support Matters'
- Opening text: 'The SEND consultation may have ended, but changes to the SEND system will now be shaped through Parliament. As proposals move through the House of Commons and House of Lords, MPs and Peers need to keep hearing directly from parents, carers, professionals and supporters'
- A clear 'Email your MP' button that takes people straight to the MP-finder tool

**What happens next?**

- Explains that the Government will review consultation responses before bringing forward its next proposals, and that any legislation must pass through both the House of Commons and House of Lords
- Reassures supporters there is still time to make sure children's needs, legal rights and access to specialist support stay part of the discussion

**Why email your MP now?**

- Explains why constituent emails carry weight with MPs
- Sets out the five key asks, numbered:
  1. Children's needs must come first
  2. Parents and young people must be heard
  3. The right support must be available in the right setting
  4. Legal rights and routes to challenge must be protected
  5. Changes to the SEND system must avoid unintended harm
- A second button: 'Ask your MP to protect children's SEND rights'

**Keep families' voices in Parliament (closing section)**

- Encourages supporters to keep emailing as changes move through Parliament and to follow updates at SENDMattersCoalition.com
- Asks people to share the page with others and on social media
- A button linking to SENDMattersCoalition.com

## The 'Email your MP' tool

People enter their postcode, the tool finds their MP, and they choose one of four ready-written letters before sending from their own email account. The four letters are:

- General supporter
- Parent of a child currently at a specialist school
- Parent trying to secure a specialist school place
- Teacher or member of school staff (this one also asks for the person's role and school name so the letter reads correctly)

Each letter uses the wording supplied, with the MP's name and the sender's details filled in automatically. People can still edit the letter in their email app before sending.

## Branding

The header and footer logo and wordmark are unchanged. The footer description and main link now reference the SEND campaign and SENDMattersCoalition.com.
